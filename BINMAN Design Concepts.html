    Where I'd put my money

    Build Split Ledger as the skeleton. It is the only idiom that survives a hundred rows per module, and the accumulating query stack is itself the audit trail — you can screenshot it into a slide and someone can reproduce the shortlist.

    Then borrow one move from Ternary Triangle: a small persistent glyph in the header showing which of the three corners (glue, E3, target) are pinned. It costs one SVG and it is the single clearest answer to "what am I currently looking at".

    Keep Lens Graph on the roadmap as a view rather than a layout. Graphs are the right picture for E3–substrate networks, but they are a terrible place to read a lysine distance table.
