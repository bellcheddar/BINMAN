{# Ternary triangle (spec 6.3): persistent, always visible, and also a control. Corners fill as they are pinned, edges light when a real structural link exists between the pinned pair, and the centre resolves a degradability readout only when all three are pinned. Drawn chunky on purpose: at the old 5.5 px radius with 2 px edges the three corners read as dots on a wireframe rather than as three things you can click. The nodes are nearly twice the radius now, the edges carry round caps and sit behind the nodes, and the labels are set below and above the shape instead of against it. The centre marker no longer draws a dashed ring when nothing is resolved: an empty dotted circle in the middle looked like a fourth node, and people tried to click it. It appears only once it has something to say. #}
