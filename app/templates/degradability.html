Honest limits of this metric. A lysine counts as unmodified here only if it was seen in an identified peptide and carried no ubiquitylation, so absence of evidence is no longer being read as evidence of absence: 419,405 lysines were assayed, 110,108 of them ubiquitylated. That mattered. On the lysines common to both sets, the old labelling and the assayed one disagree 41% of the time, nearly always on a lysine called unmodified because nobody had looked. What remains a proxy is the biology rather than the statistics. The reach geometry was measured in induced ternary complexes, but the ubiquitylation labels still come from native E3 activity, so a lysine a cell ubiquitylates is standing in for one a degrader would. Narrowed, not removed.
